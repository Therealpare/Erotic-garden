More than a garden.{{ '\n' }}More than an art space.

Chiang Mai Erotic Garden & Teahouse brings together contemporary sculpture, tropical planting and a boutique teahouse across a series of quiet, shaded terraces in Mae Rim. Art and nature are not separated here — each installation is placed to be discovered slowly, on foot, among the trees.

Visitors are invited to move at their own pace: through the garden, into the gallery spaces, and finally to rest with tea overlooking the hills.
